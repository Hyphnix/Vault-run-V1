The main idea behind VAULT RUN is to create an exciting, offline stealth game where the player takes on the role of a thief trying to rob highly secure vaults without getting caught.
🎮 The main concept
The player must sneak through different security facilities, collect valuable loot and reach the exit before time runs out. Each level becomes harder, with more dangerous security drones, tighter time limits and greater risks.
🔑 How the game works

Collect loot: Gather gold and other valuables to increase your score.
Avoid security: Sneak past drones and avoid getting caught.
Use gadgets: Sprint, dash and jam nearby drones to help you escape.
Beat the clock: Reach the exit before the alarm triggers a lockdown.
Progress through levels: Each level has its own atmosphere, colour scheme and increasing difficulty.
Build your score: Escape successfully to earn rewards and improve your career bank.
🌃 What makes it immersive?
The aim is to make the player feel as though they are actually inside a high-security facility. Background music creates tension, alarms react to danger, lighting changes the mood, and each new level introduces a different environment.
For example, the early levels feel like sneaking through a bank at night, while the later levels feel like infiltrating a futuristic maximum-security complex.
🎯 The overall goal
Take bigger risks to steal more loot, but know when to escape before everything goes wrong.
The game combines stealth, time pressure, strategy and arcade-style action. Its main appeal is the balance between greed and survival: collecting more loot earns a bigger reward, but staying longer makes escaping more dangerous.
